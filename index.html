Subtransit

A game about escaping the subway

Requirements

  • WebGL support
  • Keybooard and mouse
  • Working display
  • Working audio output
  • Internet connection

Controls

  • WASD keys to move
  • Move the mouse to look around
  • Left Click to shoot and right click to alternate fire
  • Press "E" to interact with things
  • Press "R" to reload your weapon
  • Press ESC to open the menu
